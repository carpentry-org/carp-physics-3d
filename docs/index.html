carp-physics-3d

A 3D rigid-body physics suite for Carp.

(load "git@github.com:carpentry-org/carp-physics-3d@master")

Transforms and quaternions come from carp-math, and handles from carp-collections; both are pulled in for you.

Shapes

  • Geometry — shapes and their support functions, with Plane and SDF
  • SpatialGrid — uniform grid for broad-phase pruning

Collision

  • Collision — narrow phase, producing Contacts and a ContactSet
  • CollisionResult, CollisionChecker — the result and dispatch types

Simulation

  • Body — mass, velocity and the state that integrates
  • Integrator — stepping that state forward
  • RigidBody — a body with a shape and a transform
  • Solver — the constraint solver that ties it together
